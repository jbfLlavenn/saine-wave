# Privacy

Saine Wave has no server, no account, no analytics and no ads. It doesn't collect or sell anything.

## What it stores

- **Songs and playlists** (video ID, name, start and end times, settings) are saved in your browser's sync storage. If browser sync is on, your browser provider (Microsoft for Edge, Google for Chrome) carries them between your computers, the same way it syncs your other extension data.
- **Lyrics, chosen backgrounds, romaji and English translations, and panel settings** are saved only on the computer you're using.

Removing the extension deletes all of this. Use *Settings → Backup and sharing → Export everything* first if you want to keep a copy.

## What it connects to

- **YouTube** — only the pages you open yourself. Saine Wave runs on `youtube.com` to control the player and show lyrics.
- **LRCLIB (lrclib.net)** — when you look for lyrics, the song title (and artist, if known) is sent to LRCLIB to search for matches. Nothing else is sent. See [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net) for their own policy.
- **English translation** uses the translator built into your browser. Edge may download a language model the first time; the lyrics are translated on your computer.

## Share links

A share link holds the song or playlist itself (video IDs, names and times), packed into the link. It isn't uploaded anywhere — whoever you send it to gets exactly what's in the link.
